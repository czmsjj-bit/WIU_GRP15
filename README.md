    <<HOW TO PLAY>>
Using WASD as key movement, navigate the room and push the boulder(O) by grabbing it using G key when near a boulder(0).
Pressing K on the first room will trigger a dialogue to help you better understand the game, also with Room 2, the other Rooms will not have any dialogue afterwards.
By pushing the boulder left right and forward depends on where you facing, push it towards the button to unlock the door(U) to door(N).
Going through the door(N) will bring you an encounter before the next room. You can choose to leave the Encounter or use a soul to exchange an item from the encounter.
Using a soul to exchange items will increase the enemies damage.
In rooms with Harpy(H) Enemy and Cerberus(W) Enemy, Avoid Harpy by moving away from it. 
To avoid Cerberus, use a boulder while pushing behind the boulder eg. W 0P or P0 W, will prevent damage from taken.
Find the real button and push the boulder into before enemies kills you.
